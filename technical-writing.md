## How I work

I start with the reader’s task: what they need to understand or do. I review language, logic and structure, check field and button labels against the material and separate actions from notes. Publication preparation includes markup, code and image checks.

For repetitive operations I use a [content-processing pipeline](./content-processing), while reviewing wording changes in the context of the individual article.
